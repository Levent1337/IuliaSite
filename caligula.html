Caligula

Bucharest · UNATC · 2020

Theatre Performance

In 2020, I directed Albert Camus' Caligula, at Odeon Theatre, Bucharest, aiming to bring a contemporary twist to the exploration of absolute power's moral complexities. The play immerses audiences in a dark journey through the psyche of Caligula, the Roman Emperor driven to madness and tyranny by the loss of his sister. My production has a modern perspective into Camus' existential themes, forcing viewers to confront the repercussions of unbridled authority. The aim is to offer a visceral experience, delving into the intricate dynamics of rebellion, nihilism, and the enduring existential questions that resonate in our present-day world.
