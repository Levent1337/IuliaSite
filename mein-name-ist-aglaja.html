Mein Name ist Aglaja

Berlin · TAK - Theater im Aufbau · 2024

Theatre Performance

My Name is Aglaja premiered in Berlin in April 2024, at tak Theater Aufbau Kreuzberg, Berlin. The play follows Aglaja, a woman growing up in a circus, grappling with her fears and identity as her family flees communist Romania. Blending surrealism, humor, and raw emotion, the performance explores childhood trauma, belonging, and resilience.

It is based on the autobiographical novel Warum das Kind in der Polenta kocht by Aglaja Veteranyi.
