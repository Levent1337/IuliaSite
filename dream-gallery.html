Dream Gallery

Berlin · [Ceci n'est pas une] Galerie - RKI · 2024

Immersive Performance

Dream Gallery is a performance project, debuting 23-25 March 2024 at [Ceci n'est pas une] Gallery, the art space of Rumänisches Kulturinstitut Berlin. Inspired by dreams collected from around the world, this immersive experience blends visual art, sound installation and performance to explore the connections between reality and the unconscious. It’s an invitation to rethink reality and challenge conventions.
