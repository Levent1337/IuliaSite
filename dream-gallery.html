Dream Gallery (Chapter 1 and II)

Berlin · [Ceci n'est pas une] Galerie - RKI · 2024

Immersive Performance

A participatory performance created by Iulia Grigoriu in partnership with RKI Berlin (the Romanian Cultural Institute of Berlin), staged at [Ceci n'est pas une] Gallery, Berlin, in July 2024. Conceived as a creative laboratory for performative thinking, the piece dissolves the boundary between contemporary art and spectacle: dreams gathered from around the world through an open "Call for Dreams" were folded directly into the performance's structure, letting audience submissions shape the work from the inside.
