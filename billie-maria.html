Billie & Maria

Berlin · Volksbühne · 2025

Theater Production

Billie & Maria is a theater performance directed by Iulia Grigoriu, which premiered at the Grüner Salon of Volksbühne Berlin in 2025.

The performance brings together, in an imagined encounter after death, two iconic singers who never met in their lifetimes: Billie Holiday and Maria Tănase. Played by Maimouna Sow and Ioana Elena Urda, the two women share stories about their childhoods, poverty, love, abuse, addiction, music and their lives as artists.

Although they came from different places and backgrounds, their stories reveal experiences of racism, sexism, violence and social inequality. Music becomes a way of surviving, transforming difficult experiences into a source of strength and expression.
