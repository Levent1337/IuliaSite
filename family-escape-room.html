Family Escape Room

Pitesti · Asociatia Culturala Ilfoveanu-Badea · 2023

Interactive Performance

Family Escape Room premiered in 2023 in Bucharest, Romania, following a writing residency as part of the Arts Foundation Project. This immersive black comedy is set in a four-room art gallery, where the audience is invited to freely explore and interact with the space, choosing which part of the story to see, but hearing everything through silent party headphones. The performance revolves around the children of Arthur Olsen, who must play their family’s infamous Escape Room game for the last time after their father’s death. As they uncover clues to his testament, they navigate through a transformative journey that explores the conversion of personal trauma into collective understanding.
