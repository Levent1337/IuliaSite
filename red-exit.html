RED EXIT

Spiekeroog · Spiekerooger Zeltplatzresidenz · 2024

Installation & Performance

RED EXIT! is a site-specific installation and performance by Iulia Grigoriu, created during her 2024 residency at Spiekeroog Island as part of the Zeltplatzresidenz program. The work was developed and presented on the North Sea beach in the summer of 2024.

At the centre of the installation is a five-metre-high triangular curtain made of red fabric. Standing on the beach, it creates a kind of passage between the island and the sea. A small sign next to it reads “For the Unresolved.”

The performance grew out of conversations with people living on the island and their stories of leaving things behind. The characters arrive carrying heavy suitcases and try to bury them in the sand. As the story unfolds, they discover that leaving for a distant or isolated place does not mean leaving their problems behind. What remains unresolved continues to follow them.
