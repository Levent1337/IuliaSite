RED EXIT

Spiekeroog · Spiekerooger Zeltplatzresidenz · 2024

Installation & Performance

As the winner of the 10th edition of the Spiekerooger Zeltplatzresidenz, I had the opportunity to experiment theatrically on the Nordsee beach. My project, RED EXIT, involved creating a triangular red curtain installation, as a portal to non-reality, alongside a performance inspired by the stories of Spiekeroog island's residents. The project explored themes of unresolved pasts and the quest for belonging.
