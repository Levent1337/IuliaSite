Der Elefant im Raum

Chemnitz · Fritz Theater · 2025

Teatre Performance

"The Elephant in the Room" tells a story about confronting the past and searching for a common language in a broken relationship, where prolonged silence becomes an obstacle to possible reconciliation. Two sisters, Noni and Ana, process the loss of their father in their own ways: by seeking answers in the wrong places, deflecting blame, remaining silent, and ultimately isolating themselves within their respective realities. A third figure intervenes in this dynamic: the narrator, who possesses the power to alter elements surrounding the characters—including time itself. He attempts to mend the situation, but in the end, it becomes clear that something else is at stake.

The play raises questions about the nature of unresolved conflicts and the fragility of reconciliation. "The Elephant in the Room" invites the audience to reflect on the invisible moments of our interactions—those that are essential yet often overlooked.
