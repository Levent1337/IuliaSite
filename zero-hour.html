Ora Zero

Bucharest · Teatrelli · 2026

Gaming Theater Production

Ora Zero is a hybrid gaming theatre performance combining theatre, dramaturgy, and game design, with the audience directly influencing the course of the action. Set in an illegal basement in Bucharest, the dark comedy follows a group of people who have withdrawn from the world above. Their fragile routine is disrupted by a young motivational coach whose belief in progress and easy solutions clashes with the logic of the basement.

As the audience shapes the story through its choices, Zero, one of the residents, prepares an extreme reset he believes could free humanity from suffering. At its core, Ora Zero asks an impossible ethical question: is it better to let humanity continue suffering, or end that suffering altogether?
