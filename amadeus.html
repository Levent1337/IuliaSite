Amadeus

Bucharest · UNATC · 2016

Theatre Performance

In 2016, I directed Amadeus by Peter Shaffer as my diploma show at the National University of Theatre and Film in Bucharest, aiming to offer a fresh take on the classic play. I chose to cast two Salieris: a young one from the past and the old one from the present, reflecting on a life marked by regret.

The show was invited to several festivals and received critical acclaim, winning two awards for Best Director. Its minimalist staging, along with the emotional depth of the performances, resonated strongly with audiences. The production’s success reaffirmed the timeless relevance of the play’s themes, showcasing the intense rivalry and human complexities between genius and mediocrity.
