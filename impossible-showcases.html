Impossible Showcases

Bucharest · Green Hours · 2021

Performance, Installation

In 2021, along with the pandemic challenges, I created "Impossible Showcases." Since traditional stages weren't an option, I took the audience to the streets. Using a display case as a special stage, people could get up close to the actors and hear what was happening inside through headphones. From inside, the characters shared their stories, creating a close connection despite the physical distance. My project aimed to give spectators a fresh experience, thinking outside the usual theater setting during these challenging times. As the characters shared their narratives from within the display case, the audience experienced an intimate connection, transcending physical distance..
