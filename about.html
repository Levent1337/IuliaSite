Iulia Grigoriu

Theatre Director

Theater Director & Dramaturg

ulia Grigoriu (she/her) is a 32 year old Romanian theatre director and dramaturg based in Berlin. She holds a BA and MA in Directing from the National University of Theatre and Film “I.L. Caragiale” in Bucharest and works between Romania and Germany. Her practice moves between theatre, performance, visual art and experimental formats, with a particular interest in audience participation and new forms of narrative.

Her previous work includes Caligula at Odeon Theatre, Bucharest, Mein Name ist Aglaja at TAK Theatre, Berlin, projects created for galleries and unconventional spaces, including the site-specific trilogy Impossible Showcases in Bucharest, Red Exit, a performative installation developed during the Zeltplatzresidenz 2024 on Spiekeroog Island, and Dream Gallery, a performance based on dreams at RKI Berlin. In 2025, two productions wrote and directed by Grigoriu premiered in Germany: Der Elefant im Raum at Fritz Theater, Chemnitz, and Billie & Maria at Volksbühne am Rosa-Luxemburg-Platz, Grüner Salon, Berlin.

In 2026, she wrote and directed Ora Zero at Teatrelli, Bucharest, a gaming theatre dark-comedy piece developed through a two-stage work-in-progress process. The performance places the audience inside the dramaturgy, allowing their decisions to influence the relationships and actions of the characters. She is currently developing Something Rotten, also a gaming theatre performance freely based on Hamlet, for Theaterhaus Jena, with a planned premiere at the beginning of 2027.
