Lemons Lemons Lemons Lemons Lemons

Bucharest · UNTEATRU · 2019

Theatre Performance

In 2019, I directed Sam Steiner's play Lemons Lemons Lemons Lemons Lemons at UNTEATRU, Bucharest. Set in a dystopian world where individuals are restricted to speaking only 140 words a day, the play delves into the complexities of communication, intimacy, and resistance. The story follows a couple, Bernadette and Oliver, as they navigate the profound effects of this new law on their relationship. As their ability to express themselves becomes more limited, they struggle to adapt, questioning how love and connection can survive under such constraints.
